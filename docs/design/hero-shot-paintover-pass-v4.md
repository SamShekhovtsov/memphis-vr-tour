## Historical Guardrail

Keep the domestic lane restrained for Ancient Memphis scoped around the Early Dynastic / Old Kingdom horizon:

- Mudbrick remains the construction system, but near-camera house faces should read as mud-plastered or whitewashed earthen walls.
- Imperfection comes from dust, plaster, mud daub exposure, clay pottery, wood, reed, linen, and daily use.
- Door and window openings stay small and plain, with dark interiors and simple jamb/lintel treatment.
- Clothing silhouettes remain plain linen kilts or simple sheath-like linen garments, bare feet or simple sandals, restrained hair, and minimal jewelry.

Avoid regular block-grid wallpaper, later New Kingdom/Ptolemaic temple language, fantasy color, royal regalia, copied-media composition, or extra architecture at the Main Wall-side street mouth.
