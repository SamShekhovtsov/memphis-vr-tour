## What V5 Changes

- Tightens the benchmark lens so the shot reads as an eye-height lane instead of a broad empty floor test.
- Rebalances runtime lighting: less white haze, lower exposure, stronger warm sun, cooler/darker ambient fill.
- Lets the hero ground use AO/lightmap again and adds a non-pickable V5 compacted-dust overlay.
- Darkens the large overhead cloth into dust-stained shade material so it stops glowing.
- Adds broad close-camera plaster unifier skins, lower wall dirt, dark doorway pockets, and stronger contact pools.
- Increases non-repeating ground breakup: dried silt scuffs, ruts, hairline cracks, straw scratches, and dirty wall-base patches.

## Historical Guardrail

Keep the domestic lane restrained for Ancient Memphis scoped around the Early Dynastic / Old Kingdom horizon:

- Mudbrick remains the construction system, but visible house faces should read as mud-plastered or whitewashed earthen walls.
- Imperfection comes from packed dust, plaster, mud daub exposure, clay pottery, wood, reed, linen, and daily wear.
- Door and window openings stay small, plain, and dark inside.
- Clothing silhouettes remain plain linen kilts or simple sheath-like linen garments, bare feet or simple sandals, restrained hair, and minimal jewelry.

Avoid extra house bays, later-period temple ornament, royal/fantasy costume, copied reference-media composition, and any new obstruction near the Main Wall-side street mouth.

## Acceptance

V5 is acceptable when the recaptured browser shot:

- Feels darker, warmer, and more directional without returning to heavy fog.
- Reads as dirty compacted earth, not a smooth yellow/red carpet.
- Shows stronger wall-base, doorway, post, and prop contact darkness.
- Makes close wall surfaces look plastered and hand-worn instead of panel-grid regular.
- Keeps the Main Wall-side entry open and walkable.
- Improves quality without moving the approved street layout or adding new district content.
