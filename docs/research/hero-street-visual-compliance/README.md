# Hero Street Visual Compliance And Moodboard

Purpose: keep the Hero Street v2 art pass cinematic without drifting into later-period, fantasy, or generic “ancient Egypt” design.

Scope: Ancient Memphis, Early Dynastic / Old Kingdom, with Kom el-Fakhry / Mit Rahina settlement evidence and an early Ptah / Hwt-ka-Ptah endpoint.

This folder is a working art-direction guardrail, not a runtime asset source.

## Reference Rules

- Use direct Memphis sources first for place logic, city identity, and Ptah/Mit Rahina context.
- Use Old Kingdom Giza/Saqqara/Ain Asil settlement and tomb evidence for domestic street, people, craft, and material culture.
- Treat later-period Memphis/Ptah reconstructions as endpoint mood only, not as proof for the hero street.
- Treat cinematic/AI/popular references as composition inspiration only.
- Do not ingest, copy, train on, or ship visual media unless item-level license status has been checked.

## Visual Compliance Checklist

### Architecture

- **Confirmed / Strongly evidenced:** mudbrick as the ordinary building system; flat roofs; compact rooms; small door/window openings; plastered or whitewashed surfaces; roof storage and simple thresholds.
- **Inferred:** exact house spacing, street width, facade rhythm, roof clutter, awning placement, and doorway darkness.
- **Speculative:** highly finished continuous film-set street composition, exact white-wall approach, exact view toward Ptah precinct.
- **Avoid:** exposed regular block-grid facades on every house, tall New Kingdom temple pylons inside the domestic street, fantasy obelisks/processional boulevards, oversized monumental doors on ordinary houses.

### Materials

- **Confirmed / Strongly evidenced:** mudbrick, plaster/whitewash, limestone/painted limestone in tomb/temple contexts, pottery, baskets, wood/reed/linen, packed dust.
- **Inferred:** exact plaster weathering, wall-base dirt, contact-shadow patterns, warm/cool color grading, baked lightmaps.
- **Speculative:** dense cinematic dirt choreography and hero-shot lighting.
- **Avoid:** glossy stone-like houses, saturated gold/purple palettes, clean modern concrete, tiled floor reads, obvious repeating procedural patterns.

### People And Clothing

- **Confirmed / Strongly evidenced:** plain linen kilts for many men; simple sheath-like linen garments for women; bare feet or simple sandals; restrained hair silhouettes; minimal jewelry for ordinary daily street life.
- **Inferred:** exact NPC routines, market behaviors, carrying poses, shade-cloth work areas.
- **Speculative:** exact conversations, precise social mix, choreographed route-side activity.
- **Avoid:** New Kingdom royal collars/crowns, elaborate fantasy robes, armored figures, dramatic ritual costumes in the residential lane.

### Street And Ground

- **Confirmed / Strongly evidenced:** packed earth/dust, practical walking surfaces, domestic debris, pottery, baskets, tools, bread/beer/craft activity from tomb scenes.
- **Inferred:** footprints, straw, ruts, swept piles, cracked dust, wall-base grime.
- **Speculative:** exact density and arrangement of foreground objects.
- **Avoid:** too many objects that block the walking route, grid-like ground texture, bright orange/yellow carpet effect, clean empty street.

### Lighting And Atmosphere

- **Confirmed / Strongly evidenced:** strong Egyptian sun, shade as a practical necessity, deep interior/doorway contrast.
- **Inferred:** warm low sun for cinematic route timing, cooler shadows, amber dust haze, baked contact shadows.
- **Speculative:** exact time of day and filmic color grade.
- **Avoid:** white fog washing out the scene, flat no-direction lighting, over-dark browser view, uniform sepia palette.
